Battle Line

Goal: Claim 5 flags total, or 3 adjacent flags.

Turn: Play one card to your side of any unclaimed flag (max 3 per side), then draw.

Formations (high → low):

Wedge3 consecutive, same colour
Phalanx3 of the same value
Battalion3 of the same colour
Skirmish3 consecutive, any colour
Hostany 3 (sum decides)

Claim: A "Claim" button appears on flags where no formation the opponent could build from any unplayed card (deck + both hands) can beat yours. Your own hand isn't used to narrow this pool — you can't disclose private cards to prove a claim.
